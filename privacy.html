Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2025-12-17

This is a demo. Don’t post sensitive personal data. Content you submit may be visible to other users depending on where you post it.

What this demo is

BeAwarely is an experimental demo. Features, storage and moderation rules may change as development continues.

Data we may process

  • Account data you provide (e.g. email, username, profile fields).
  • User content you submit (e.g. posts, tool ideas, WorkVerify entries, Faith & Reason items, chat messages where applicable).
  • Technical data needed to run and secure the service (e.g. basic logs, error reports, timestamps, abuse-prevention signals).

Why we process data

  • To provide login and core features of the platform.
  • To display your content inside the app where relevant.
  • To prevent abuse/spam and keep the service stable and secure.
  • To debug issues and improve reliability.

Where data is stored

BeAwarely uses Supabase for authentication and database storage.

Cookies / local storage

The site may use browser storage (e.g. localStorage / sessionStorage) to keep you logged in or remember basic UI state (for example theme or redirects).

Data retention

Demo data may be removed, reset, or migrated at any time during development.

Your choices

  • You can stop using the service at any time.
  • If you want your data removed, contact the admin email below and include your account email/username.

Contact

Questions or data removal requests: admin@beawarely.com
